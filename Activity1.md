## 🩷 01 · Visual Hierarchy

I applied **visual hierarchy** by giving greater emphasis to titles, headings, and important information. This helped guide the viewer's attention and made the main ideas easier to identify.

<div align="center">

<img src="https://img.shields.io/badge/VISUAL%20HIERARCHY-FCE4EC?style=for-the-badge&labelColor=F8D4E2&color=FCE4EC">

</div>

<br>

## 💜 02 · Balance

I used **balance** to distribute text, images, and other visual elements across each slide. This prevented the presentation from looking crowded and created a more comfortable viewing experience.

<div align="center">

<img src="https://img.shields.io/badge/BALANCE-EDE3F5?style=for-the-badge&labelColor=DCC8EB&color=EDE3F5">

</div>

<br>

## 🩷 03 · Alignment

I applied **alignment** to organize the content and maintain a clean structure. Consistent positioning of text and visual elements made the slides easier to follow.

<div align="center">

<img src="https://img.shields.io/badge/ALIGNMENT-FCE4EC?style=for-the-badge&labelColor=F8D4E2&color=FCE4EC">

</div>

<br>

## 💜 04 · Contrast

I used **contrast** to emphasize important information and separate different elements. Differences in size, color, and emphasis helped improve readability and visual interest.

<div align="center">

<img src="https://img.shields.io/badge/CONTRAST-EDE3F5?style=for-the-badge&labelColor=DCC8EB&color=EDE3F5">

</div>

<br>

## 🩷 05 · Consistency

I maintained **consistency** by using similar typography, colors, spacing, and design elements throughout the presentation. This helped create a cohesive and professional visual identity.

<div align="center">

<img src="https://img.shields.io/badge/CONSISTENCY-FCE4EC?style=for-the-badge&labelColor=F8D4E2&color=FCE4EC">

</div>

<br>

## 💜 06 · Simplicity

I applied **simplicity** by avoiding unnecessary elements and focusing on the most relevant information. This allowed the audience to understand the message without visual distractions.

<div align="center">

<img src="https://img.shields.io/badge/SIMPLICITY-EDE3F5?style=for-the-badge&labelColor=DCC8EB&color=EDE3F5">

</div>

</div>

<br>

Applying these principles made my presentation **more organized, readable, balanced, and visually engaging**.

Visual hierarchy helped highlight important information, while balance and alignment created a cleaner layout. Contrast helped key details stand out, and consistency created a unified appearance across the slides. Simplicity also helped keep the presentation focused and easy to understand.
